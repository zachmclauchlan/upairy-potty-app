**Description (4,000 max)**

Potty Up walks you through potty training one day at a time, so you always know what to do next.

Pick the method that suits your family, and Potty Up turns it into a simple daily plan: what to try today, when to offer the potty, and when your child is ready for the next stage.

A PLAN FOR EVERY DAY
• Choose your method, including a 3-day intensive
• One short lesson each day, with clear steps to tick off
• A quick evening check-in that tells you whether to move on or stay a little longer
• A readiness check before you start

KNOW WHEN TO OFFER THE POTTY
• A timer that counts down to the next try
• It learns your child's own rhythm from your logs, like the usual gap between wees and how soon after a drink
• Gentle reminders, with quiet hours for naps and bedtime
• One-tap logging for wees, poos, accidents, drinks, meals and wake-ups

ASK LARA, YOUR POTTY COACH
• Ask anything, any time: refusing to sit, accidents, poo worries, regression, daycare
• Answers shaped by your child's age, stage and recent days
• Built-in guides for the most common situations

MADE FUN FOR KIDS
• A sticker chart that fills up with every success
• Stars to save and spend on rewards you choose
• 20 potty songs with sing-along lyrics, and a sit timer with music
• Kid mode, so little hands can only tap the fun parts

SEE THE PROGRESS
• Success rate by day, accidents, dry nights and how often your child tells you first
• A night mode for going nappy-free at night
• A ready-to-send update for daycare or grandparents

YOURS, AND PRIVATE
• No ads and no tracking
• Your child's logs stay on your phone, with an optional private backup to your account so they move to a new phone
• Export or delete everything at any time

GETTING STARTED
Tap Get started and set up your child in two minutes. No account needed.
Have an UpAiry Potty Up membership? Sign in with the email from your order plus your order number, postcode or last name to add Lara's AI answers and a private backup. No password needed.

Potty Up gives general guidance, not medical advice.

## App Privacy answers

"Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?" **Yes.**

For every type below: used for tracking **No**; linked to the user's identity **Yes**.

| Data type (Apple's name) | What it is in Potty Up | Purpose to tick |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info > Email Address | The order email used to sign in | App Functionality |
| Identifiers > User ID | The account's ID | App Functionality |
| Purchases > Purchase History | Whether the account has an active Potty Up membership, and the linked order number | App Functionality |
| User Content > Other User Content | The backup of the family's logs and settings (when backup is on), and questions typed to the coach | App Functionality |
| Usage Data > Product Interaction | When setup was finished and which days the app was opened | Analytics |
| Diagnostics > Crash Data | Error reports when a screen fails | App Functionality |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, health records, browsing or search history, advertising data, payment details.

## Uploading the app

1. Testing > Internal testing > Create new release.
2. When asked about app signing, choose "Use Google-generated key" (Play App Signing).
3. Upload the `.aab` file.
4. Release name: 1.0.0. Release notes: "First release."
5. Save, then Review release, then Start rollout to internal testing.
6. When you're happy, go to Production > Create new release > "Add from library", pick the same build, and send it for review.
