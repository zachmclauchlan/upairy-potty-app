## Full description (both stores, max 4,000)

Potty training, one calm step at a time.

Potty Up gives you a day-by-day plan for your toddler, a timer that learns their rhythm, and a coach you can ask anything. It's made by UpAiry, the potty training underwear brand from New Zealand.

PICK YOUR METHOD
- 3-day intensive
- Bare bottom at home
- Gentle, child-led
- Nights and dry mornings
Not sure they're ready? Take the 8-question readiness check first.

A PLAN THAT ADAPTS
Each step has a clear goal. A quick evening check-in tells Potty Up how the day went. Then it moves you on, repeats the day, or steps back. No guesswork.

A TIMER THAT LEARNS
Log wees, poos, tries and accidents in one tap. Potty Up learns your child's pattern and times reminders to suit it. Set quiet hours for naps and nights. Forgot to log? It's forgiving.

REMINDERS THAT HELP
Gentle nudges before it's time to go. Log right from the notification with "Wee", "Nothing" or "Accident" buttons.

AN AI POTTY COACH
Stuck on poo refusal, regression or daycare? Ask the Potty Up coach. It's built on UpAiry's own potty training approach and looks at your logs to give practical steps for today.

FOR YOUR TODDLER
Kid mode, potty songs, stars, stickers and rewards you choose.

TRACK PROGRESS
History, streaks, a calendar and night tracking. Share a ready-to-send update with daycare or grandparents.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your child's logs stay on your phone. No ads, no tracking.

Potty Up gives general guidance, not medical advice. If you're worried about your child's health, talk to your doctor or nurse.

## Naming notes

- **Name:** "Potty Up". In copy it's "Potty Up by UpAiry".
- **Status words:** the paid version is just "Potty Up". The app says "unlocked" or "free version". It never says membership, member, VIP or Plus.
- **Shopify product:** the product in the cart should be called **Potty Up** (like PureFlow's ImReady), billed through Loop.
- **Domain:** pottyup.com is taken. The app can live at app.upairy.com, with a landing page at upairy.com/pages/potty-up.
- **Trademark:** before launch, run a trademark search for "Potty Up" in class 9 (apps) in NZ (IPONZ), Australia and the US. Watch for Kimberly-Clark's "Potty with Pull-Ups".
