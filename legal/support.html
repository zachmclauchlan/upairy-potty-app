Help with the Potty Up app by UpAiry.

Signing in

  • Use the email from your upairy.com order, then one of: the order number, the postcode your order went to, or your last name. The order number is in your confirmation email and starts with #. You can type it with or without the #.
  • They must match the details on that order. If it doesn't work, check for typos in the email, or tap Help me log in on the sign-in screen.
  • After 8 wrong tries, sign-in is paused for an hour for your security.

I bought Potty Up but the app says "free"

Make sure you signed in with the email you used at checkout. Then go to Settings → Check my purchase.

New phone

If Back up to my account is on (Settings), signing in on your new phone restores your child's data automatically.

Reminders aren't arriving

  • Check that notifications are allowed for Potty Up in your phone's settings.
  • Check quiet hours and Pause in the app.
  • Open the app at least once every few days so reminders stay scheduled.

Cancel or manage your subscription

Log in to your account on upairy.com. Cancelling during the 7-day free trial means you won't be charged.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete my account and data. This permanently deletes:

  • your account;
  • your cloud backup;
  • the data on that phone.

Without the app: send a request through our contact page from the email on your account, with the subject "Delete my Potty Up account". We'll delete your account and backup within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting your Potty Up account doesn't cancel a subscription on upairy.com. Please cancel that separately.
