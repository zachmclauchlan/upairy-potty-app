Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how UpAiry ("we", "us") handles information in the Potty Up app (iPhone, Android and web). It applies alongside the UpAiry store privacy policy, which covers purchases on upairy.com.

The short version

  • Your child's potty logs live on your phone. If backup is on, a copy is saved to your account so it can move to a new phone. You can turn backup off at any time.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising SDKs in the app.
  • The AI coach receives your question and an anonymous summary of your child's progress. It never receives your child's name.
  • You can export your data or delete your account and everything with it from inside the app.

What we collect and why

Your account. Your email address, and whether you have Potty Up. We use them to sign you in and unlock the app. When you sign in with an order number, we check that order against your email with our subscription provider. We don't keep the order number on our servers.

Your child's app data. This is what you enter in the app: your child's first name or nickname, age range, the words they use, the training method and step, potty logs (wees, poos, accidents, drinks, meals, wake-ups and nights), evening check-ins and notes, rewards, settings, and your conversation with the coach. It is stored on your device.

  • Cloud backup (on by default, can be turned off): a copy of this data is saved to your account so you can restore it on a new phone. Only you can read your backup when signed in. Turning backup off in Settings deletes the saved copy.

AI coach. When you ask the coach a question, we send your question, your recent coach messages and an anonymous summary of your child's progress (for example, method, step, and counts of wees and accidents) to our AI provider to write an answer. We do not send your child's name or your email. We don't keep a copy of your coach conversations on our servers; they stay in the app (and in your backup if it's on). We store a daily count of how many questions you've asked, to apply the daily limit.

Purchases. Potty Up is bought on upairy.com. Your payment details are handled by Shopify and our subscription provider, and the app never sees them.

Reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device. We don't use push-notification servers or collect a notification token.

What we don't collect: location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising IDs, or your child's full name or date of birth.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. Each only processes data to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: accounts, backup and server functions. Data is hosted on Amazon Web Services in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Anthropic: the AI model that writes coach answers. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Loop Subscriptions and Shopify: subscription billing and checking whether you have Potty Up.
  • Expo: app delivery and updates.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell or rent personal information.

Children

Potty Up is designed for parents and carers, not for children to use alone (kid mode is used together with a parent). Child-related data is entered by the parent, minimised (a first name or nickname is enough), and controlled by the parent, who can export or delete it at any time.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Your account and backup: kept until you delete your account. Go to Settings → Delete my account and data. This permanently deletes your account, your backup and the data on that phone. You can also ask us to delete your account through the Potty Up support page.
  • Export: Settings → Export my data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, the Australian Privacy Act, UK and EU GDPR, or US state laws), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and complain to your privacy regulator. Contact us through the UpAiry contact page and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Backups are protected so that only your signed-in account can read them. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device protected.

Health information

Potty logs can include health-related information about your child. We use it only to run the app's features for you.

Changes

If we make significant changes, we'll update this page and tell you in the app.
